About Us

Learn more about Grocer! and our mission

Our Story

Grocer! was born out of a simple frustration — standing in the middle of a supermarket aisle, trying to remember whether you needed coriander or cumin, and realising the shopping list was still stuck to the fridge at home. We set out to build a smarter, simpler way for people to plan their grocery shopping, keep track of what they need, and never miss a deal again.

Founded in 2024, our small team of food lovers and tech enthusiasts came together with one goal: to make grocery shopping less of a chore and more of an experience. Whether you are meal-prepping for the week or grabbing last-minute essentials, Grocer! keeps you organised and in control.

What We Do

Grocer! helps you create and manage personalised grocery lists, discover deals at your favourite stores, and find nearby shops so you always know where to go. Our platform brings together everything you need for a stress-free shopping trip — all in one place.

Why Choose Grocer!?

Smart Lists

Create multiple grocery lists, add items on the fly, and check them off as you shop. No more forgotten ingredients or duplicate purchases.

Best Deals

We track promotions and discounts across local supermarkets so you can stretch your budget further. Fresh produce, pantry staples, snacks — we have got you covered.

Nearby Stores

Quickly find grocery stores in your area, check their hours, and plan your route. Spend less time driving around and more time enjoying your meals.

Our Mission

We believe that good food starts with good planning. Our mission is to empower every household to shop smarter, waste less, and eat better. From seasonal vegetables to everyday essentials, Grocer! is your trusted companion in the kitchen and beyond.

We are constantly working to bring you new features — recipe integration, nutritional tracking, and shared family lists are just around the corner. Stay tuned and happy shopping!
